# Dataset and Data Sources

The local inventory includes 88 Strata manifest records (synthetic) and 10 Gritworks PDFs. Twenty-three eligible scans were processed, 19 have same-stem PDF text references, and four police-report scans have no paired PDF. Gritworks is inventory-only. The sample is small, synthetic, and not representative of real-world insurance.

FUNSD is stored separately at `datasets/external/funsd/`. Its 50 testing images are used in the supplementary form OCR study; 149 training pages are not used for that evaluation. FUNSD is a general form dataset, not insurance data.

Original datasets remain in place and are not duplicated into this submission package. See project dataset notes for provenance and licensing. External datasets may require separate instructor-approved access.
